Community Guidelines

Effective Date: 28/06/2025
Last Updated: 28/06/2025

Welcome to the Climenro Forum and Climate Intelligence Platform — a place for climate researchers, students, policymakers, and citizens to engage in meaningful discussion on climate issues, policies, and solutions.

Our goal is to cultivate a civil, insightful, and inclusive community where facts matter, ideas are respected, and public debate leads to progress.

By using the Climenro platform (including dashboards, articles, and forums), you agree to abide by these guidelines.

1. Stay Focused on Climate and Policy

Climenro is a focused platform. Please keep discussions, articles, and questions relevant to:

  • Climate science, adaptation, and mitigation
  • Emission trends and policy analysis
  • Renewable energy, infrastructure, agriculture, and related sectors
  • Global and national net-zero efforts
  • Environmental justice and sustainability transitions

Off-topic content may be removed.

2. Be Respectful and Constructive

We welcome disagreement, but not disrespect.

Please:

  • Critique ideas, not individuals
  • Avoid sarcasm, ridicule, or personal attacks
  • Assume good intent and engage in good faith
  • Use clear, calm, and fact-based language

We reserve the right to warn or ban users who consistently disrupt respectful discourse.

3. Back Your Claims

Given the scientific nature of our community:

  • Always cite credible sources (e.g., peer-reviewed articles, verified datasets, government reports)
  • Avoid misinformation, unverified theories, or manipulated statistics
  • Be transparent about uncertainties and limitations

This is critical to maintaining the integrity of our data-driven ecosystem.

4. Share High-Quality Content

We value thoughtful contributions:

  • When posting an article or policy insight, aim for clarity, evidence, and originality
  • Avoid clickbait titles, low-effort posts, or content generated purely by automation/AI without human review
  • When commenting, aim to add value — not just agreement or opposition

Great content helps everyone grow.

5. Protect Privacy and Identity

  • Do not post private or personally identifiable information (PII) about others
  • Use caution when discussing sensitive policy debates — especially those tied to vulnerable communities
  • You may use a pseudonym, but impersonation of others is prohibited

We comply with Canadian privacy laws and expect the same from users.

6. Prohibited Content

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Hate speech, discriminatory or inflammatory content
  • Harassment, threats, doxxing, or incitement to violence
  • Spam, self-promotion, or irrelevant advertising
  • Malicious links, malware, or phishing attempts
  • Content that violates any laws or Climenro's Terms of Service
Violations may result in warnings, content removal, or permanent suspension.

7. Forum and Dashboard Etiquette

  • Use the appropriate category or tag when posting
  • Avoid duplicate posts — search first
  • Use descriptive titles (e.g., "India's Solar Expansion Impact, 2024")
  • Flag inappropriate content using the built-in tools or notify moderators

The better we organize, the more useful our knowledge becomes.

8. Moderator Rights

Climenro moderators may:

  • Remove or edit content that violates these guidelines
  • Suspend or ban users for repeated or severe violations
  • Lock threads or disable replies in inflammatory or sensitive discussions
  • Make judgment calls in the interest of platform safety and focus

Moderators act to protect the integrity and mission of Climenro.

9. Be a Contributor, Not Just a Critic

We encourage users to:

  • Ask thoughtful questions
  • Post insightful replies and commentary
  • Write analytical pieces in the Articles & Insights section
  • Share personal or regional perspectives on climate response
  • Support newcomers in navigating the platform

You help shape this community.

10. Help Us Improve

We're still building. If you have feedback, suggestions, or concerns:

  • Reach us at: wecare@climenro.org
  • Use the "Report Issue" button on the site or submit through our Feedback Form

Final Word: Lead With Purpose

The climate challenge is one of the greatest in human history. This space exists so we can think, build, and act better — together.

Be part of the solution.

Contribute with clarity.

Engage with respect.

— The Climenro Team
