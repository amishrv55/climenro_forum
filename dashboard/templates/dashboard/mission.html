🎯 Our Mission

At Climenro, our mission is to **democratize climate policy intelligence**. We aim to make complex climate data understandable and usable for anyone making decisions about the planet—whether you're a policy maker, activist, academic, or citizen.

We believe that by breaking silos between data, governance, and technology, we can accelerate the world’s transition toward **evidence-based climate action**.

🌱 Our Goals

  • Translate climate policy and emissions data into actionable insights.
  • Support local and national governments with open-source dashboards and tools.
  • Enable collaboration between technology and policy experts.
