What is a Policy Knowledge Graph?

The Policy Knowledge Graph is an interactive, metadata-driven visualization of relationships between national policies. It helps researchers, analysts, and policymakers understand how policies evolve, relate, and influence one another over time.

Network of Policy Relationships

A knowledge graph is a network of entities (policies) connected by relationships, enabling structured exploration and analysis.

Why is this Important?

Policy Evolution

Visual summary of policy development over time

Identify Relationships

Spot amendments, revisions, and overlapping scopes

Data-Driven Research

Enables evidence-based policy analysis and comparison

Transparency

Provides structured access to public governance

Components of the Graph

Policy A

Nodes: Represent Policies

  • Policy Representation

    Each node represents a unique policy document

  • Labeling

    Labeled with short abbreviations derived from policy titles

  • Interactivity

    Hover to see full name; click to view detailed metadata

Edges: Define Relationships

  • Revision Relationship

    revision_of: One policy updates another

  • Amendment Relationship

    amendment_of: Indicates an official amendment

  • Thematic Relationship

    related_to: Policies sharing thematic similarity

Connection strength is defined by a similarity score visible on hover

How the Policy Graph Works

1. Policy Ingestion

Metadata is extracted from policy documents using NLP tools

2. Policy Comparison

Policies are compared based on title similarity, policy tools, intent tags, sectors, etc.

3. Visualization

Nodes and edges are rendered dynamically using D3.js

Fuzzy Logic Matching

Identifies relationships even with imperfect matches

Temporal Precedence

Uses date-based hierarchy to determine relationship direction
