Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 28/06/2025
Last Updated: 28/06/2025

Climenro Technologies Inc. ("Climenro", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our platform, which includes the Climenro web app, data dashboards, public forum, article publishing features, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By accessing or using Climenro, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

Climenro is a Canadian-incorporated climate intelligence platform that provides interactive tools, public forums, and data dashboards to help individuals, researchers, and policymakers understand and debate climate change and policy effectiveness.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal and non-personal information:

a. Account & Identity Information

When you register an account or interact with the Forum:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Profile photo (if uploaded)
  • Organization or affiliation (optional)

b. Usage Data

When you use our dashboards or visit our platform:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device and operating system
  • Pages viewed and features used
  • Timestamps of visits
  • Session duration and clicks

c. Content Contributions

If you post content on our Forum or Articles section:

  • Text, files, images, or links you share
  • Public comments, replies, and likes

d. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Maintain session state
  • Improve performance and personalization
  • Analyze usage patterns

You can control cookie settings in your browser preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide access to dashboards, visualizations, and tools
  • Facilitate account creation and login
  • Enable public forum discussions and content submissions
  • Improve the usability, performance, and security of the platform
  • Communicate updates, research insights, or relevant events (if you opt in)
  • Generate anonymized insights for analytics and reporting
  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We may share your data under the following circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, or authentication (e.g., Streamlit, AWS, Discourse)
  • If required by law, such as responding to legal process, court orders, or governmental requests
  • In a business transfer, such as merger or acquisition (with notice to you)
  • With your explicit consent, for example if you opt in to external research collaborations

All such third parties are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your information.

5. Data Storage and Retention

All personal data is stored on secure servers, primarily located in Canada or jurisdictions with equivalent privacy protections.

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your account or data at any time (see Section 8).

6. Security Measures

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Password hashing
  • Access control and logging
  • Regular software patching and threat assessments

However, no system is completely secure. You use Climenro at your own risk.

7. Children's Privacy

Climenro is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children without verifiable parental consent.

8. Your Rights and Choices (Canada)

As a user under Canadian law (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct or update your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Request deletion of your data or account
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you believe your data rights are violated

To exercise your rights, contact us at: wecare@climenro.org

9. External Links and Integrations

Climenro may contain links to third-party platforms (e.g., Discourse Forum, external research articles). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing any personal data.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted here with the effective date. If changes are significant, we may notify users via email or in-app message.

11. Contact Us
