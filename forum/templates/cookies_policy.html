Cookies Policy

Effective Date: 25/07/2025
Last Updated: 25/07/2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Climenro uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our platform. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences over time.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Enable core platform functionality
  • Understand how users engage with the platform
  • Remember your preferences and login details
  • Improve site performance and analytics

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for platform functionality and secure login.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our pages.
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your choices and enhance your experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features may rely on third-party services (e.g., analytics or embedded content). These providers may set their own cookies to analyze usage or personalize content.

5. How You Can Control Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookie settings. You can:

  • Delete cookies stored on your device
  • Block cookies from being set in the future
  • Adjust settings per site or per session

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our platform.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Cookies Policy as technology or regulations evolve. All updates will be posted on this page with revised dates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our use of cookies, please reach out:

  • Email: wecare@climenro.org

Your Privacy Matters

We use cookies thoughtfully and transparently to improve your experience and protect your data.

You’re always in control of your privacy.

Manage your settings anytime.
