📄 About This Module

This module is part of the Climenro Climate Intelligence Platform and provides insights into electricity generation infrastructure using the Global Power Plant Database (WRI).

🔍 What It Covers:

  • National-level generation mix and installed capacity
  • Fuel mix trends and evolution over time
  • Efficiency of generation – actual vs estimated
  • Location-based mapping of plants

🧪 Methodology:

Plant-level data is aggregated by country. Metrics are derived from:

  • capacity_mw, primary_fuel, commissioning_year
  • generation_gwh, estimated_generation_gwh, latitude, longitude

🌍 Significance:

  • Helps assess national reliance on fossil vs renewable energy
  • Informs policymakers, researchers, and infrastructure planners
  • Highlights inefficiencies and under-utilized capacity

✅ How to Use:

  1. Select a country from the dropdown on the top of each page
  2. Navigate through the views: capacity, mix, efficiency, maps
  3. Use visual insights for reporting, planning, or analysis

Data Source: Global Power Plant Database, World Resources Institute (WRI)
