🌍 About Climenro

Climenro is a next-generation platform focused on enabling advanced climate policy intelligence through data, AI, and public policy insights. We empower governments, researchers, and communities with accessible tools and dashboards to understand climate trends, evaluate policies, and accelerate action.

We bring together open data, machine learning, and domain knowledge to decode climate actions and their impacts in ways that are understandable and actionable.

🔬 What Makes Us Different

  • Bridging technology with real-world policy applications.
  • Built with transparency and accessibility in mind.
  • Rooted in scientific integrity and public interest.
