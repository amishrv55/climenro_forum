🌍 Emission Intelligence

Explore greenhouse gas emissions with powerful visual insights across countries, sectors, and gases. This dashboard provides three key modules:

📘 Module 1: GHG Emissions & Sectoral Insights

This module allows users to explore GHG emissions trends, sectoral contributions, and top emitters for a specific country.

  • 📋 Country Summary: Summary of CO₂, CH₄, N₂O emissions with global rank
  • 📈 Emission Trends: GHG trend over time for selected country
  • 🟦🟩🟥 Gas-specific Emission Views: Fossil CO₂, Bio CO₂, CH₄, N₂O with top sectors
Go to Module 1

🏭 Module 2: Sector-Wise Emission Insights

This module compares GHGs across economic sectors, countries, and gases using IPCC 2006 classification.

  • 🔍 Sector Summary: Summary of emissions and ranks by sector
  • 📈 Gas-wise Trends: Emissions trend by gas for a sector
  • 📘 Sector Activity Guide: Understanding emission sources by sector
Go to Module 2

🌐 Module 3: Country Comparisons

This module enables users to compare emission trends, sector-wise outputs, and stacked sector breakdowns across countries.

  • 📈 Emission Trends: Compare total GHG emissions over time for multiple countries
  • 🏭 Sector Comparison: Sector-specific emission comparison across countries
  • 📊 Sectoral Profiles: Country-wise emission distribution by sectors (Radar)
  • 📊 Stacked Sector Breakdown: Country-wise stacked sectoral emissions
  • 📊 Deep Insights: Deep Insights into Country-wise and Sectoral Benchmarks
📈 Emission Trend 🏭 Sector Comparison 📊 Radar Profiles 📊 Stacked Sectors 📊 Deep Insights & Benchmarks

📂 Data Source: EDGAR v6.0 GHG Dataset | Methodology: IPCC 2006 Sectoral Classification
