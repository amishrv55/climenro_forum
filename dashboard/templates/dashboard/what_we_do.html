🌐 What We Do

Climenro is building a powerful open-source climate policy intelligence platform that combines AI, data science, and policy insights to decode and democratize climate information.

📊 Our Key Offerings

  • Interactive Dashboards: Visualize emissions, policies, sector comparisons, and climate trends for countries and regions worldwide.
  • Climate Knowledge Graph: A structured graph that connects climate policies, impacts, targets, and actions for intelligent querying and analysis.
  • Policy Intelligence Tools: Data pipelines and tools to compare, benchmark, and simulate climate actions across different nations and sectors.
  • Open Data + Research: All of our insights are built on openly accessible data for use by researchers, policy makers, and the public.

💡 Who Can Use Climenro?

  • Policy Makers & Governments
  • Climate Researchers & Analysts
  • Journalists, Activists & NGOs
  • Students & Educators

We’re here to support anyone who believes in evidence-based climate action.
